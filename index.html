About Blade Masters

A friendly local hairdresser the whole family can use

Blade Masters is a family hairdresser in Blenheim Central, doing the simple thing well: a good cut at a fair price, for women, men and kids. Over 20 years behind the chair means a steady, experienced hand, whether it is a full restyle or a quick clipper cut.
