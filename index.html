About Blade Masters

A proper barbershop, run by people who care about the cut

Blade Masters is a local Blenheim barbershop built on the simple stuff done well — a clean chair, a sharp blade and a cut you actually leave happy with. Whether it's a weekly tidy-up, a school-ball fade or the full hot-towel shave, you get the same steady, unhurried service.

Sit down, tell us what you're after, and we'll sort the rest. First time or a twenty-year regular, you're welcome in the chair.

  • Locally owned and run in Blenheim
  • Walk-ins welcome — bookings just as easy
  • Clean tools, clean lines, tidy finish every time
